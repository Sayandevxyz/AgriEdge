# AgriEdge Privacy & Data Minimization Policy

> **Core Principle:** Farmers own their agricultural data.

---

## 1. Geographic Privacy & Hotspot Aggregation
- **No Individual GPS Disclosure:** High-precision GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude) captured during farm onboarding are strictly restricted to the individual farmer's private account.
- **Village/Block Centroids:** Public and FPO regional disease maps aggregate cases to **Village, Block, or District centroids**.
- **K-Anonymity Threshold:** A minimum cluster threshold is enforced before an outbreak incident is visually rendered on the regional map.

---

## 2. Telemetry & Analytics Minimization
- Farm telemetry (pump runtimes, water volumes, disease symptoms) is aggregated into anonymized seasonal cohorts.
- No commercial re-selling of farmer personal identifiable information (PII) to input chemical corporations or speculative commodity trading desks.

---

## 3. Account Deletion & Data Portability
- Farmers retain the right to export all historical farm soil records, weather logs, and advisory histories in open CSV/JSON formats.
- Deleting an account executes a cascade deletion across all associated farm boundaries and uploaded imagery.
